The arena

Your room is the stadium

Dusty Gulch opens in front of you, pinned wherever you put it. Shrink it onto a coffee table or blow it up across the living room, then leave it there all evening.

Put it anywhere

Move it, turn it, size it, then tap Done and it stays. Need more room mid-match? Resize sits right there on the match bar.

Any size you like

Tabletop or room-scale, the whole bowl scales with it: banks, ramps, fence and every loose barrel on the dirt.

Everything is physical

Trucks climb the banks, boot crates across the floor and knock the bucking chutes apart a rail at a time. Everyone sees the mess land in the same place.

Controls

Hands down. Literally.

No arms held out in front of your face. Rest them in your lap and drive the whole match from there.

leftright

Steer

Pinch with one hand and slide it right or left. A live slider shows how far you are turning, and an arrow on the ground ahead of the truck points where you are headed.

Fire

Pinch with the other hand. Hold it down with a rifle and it keeps firing until you let go.

  • Left or right handed. Swap which hand steers and which one fires in Settings, along with how sharply it turns.
  • Tooltips while you learn. A hint floats by each index finger until you want it gone.
  • Practice first. A guided lap teaches steering, dodging and weapons before anyone shoots back.

The garage

Pick your ride

Three trucks, four paint jobs. Your colour follows you onto the scoreboard, the name over your roof and the ring around your photo.

Rancher

The one everybody starts in. Roll bar, stacks, and a bed wide enough to carry whatever the last box handed you.

Iron Horse

A steam locomotive on four tyres. The weapon rides the boiler, right between the smokestack and the cab.

Stagecoach

Tall, lantern-lit and top-heavy, with the luggage strapped on the roof doing duty as a gun rack.

Pick-ups

Drive through the box

Mystery boxes sit out on the borders of the dirt. Clip one and you get whatever is inside, with a few shots to spend before it is gone.

Weapon

Missile

Straight ahead, one shot. Whatever it hits is out of the match until it respawns.

Weapon

Freeze

Three missiles fan out. Each one locks a truck in ice for a few seconds, free for anybody to ram.

Weapon

Rifle

Fourteen rounds mounted on the flanks. Tap for a burst, hold to keep it chewing through health.

Hazard

Boost pads

Hit the arrows on the centre ramps at full speed for 70% more of it. Enough to clear the boxcar, and to flatten whoever you land on.

Playing together

Bring friends, or bots

Play with friends

Start SharePlay on a FaceTime call and the lobby opens for everyone on it. Take a free seat, pick free-for-all or two teams of two, drag players between sides and roll out once everybody has placed their arena.

  • A card each, with your colour, your name and a crown for the host
  • Teammates are safe from your shots and your bumper
  • Matches run 3, 5 or 7 minutes, then straight into the next round

Play on your own

Fill the empty seats with bots that drive, grab boxes, fire back and finish on the scoreboard like anyone else. Or take the practice lap and learn the arena with nobody watching.

  • Add or send away a bot from any seat
  • Same colours, same rules, same results
  • Game Center keeps your kills, your wins and your achievements
